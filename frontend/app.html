Load Simulator — autoscaling demo

load · by loadgen (Node)

Generates real CPU load against the facts service. The autoscaler (HPA) notices within ~15–30s and adds facts pods; they drain away about a minute after the load stops.

Nothing else to configure — pick a duration and concurrency, press Start Load, and watch kubectl -n k8slab get hpa -w in a terminal.

How long the load runs — HPA samples CPU every ~15s, so 60s+ gives the full demo.

Requests in flight at once — the intensity dial that drives CPU up; 20 is a good show.
